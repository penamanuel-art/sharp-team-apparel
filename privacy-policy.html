Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Sharp Team ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, purchase from The Sharp Team Apparel, create a member account, or subscribe to our text message alerts.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information — your name, email address, and mobile phone number, when you create an account, place an order, or opt in to text alerts.
  • Order information — the products you purchase, your shipping address, and order details needed to fulfill your order. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Account information — your login credentials and account preferences.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as pages visited and device/browser information, used to operate and improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and ship your orders and provide customer support.
  • To operate your member account and deliver the content and notifications you signed up for.
  • To send text message alerts to subscribers who have opted in (see the SMS section below).
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, and service updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

SMS Text Message Program

If you opt in, The Sharp Team sends text message alerts to subscribers about their member account and daily content updates. We collect your mobile number and your opt-in consent for this program. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How We Share Information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate our business — such as payment processors, shipping carriers, and messaging providers — and only as needed for them to perform those services. We may disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Your Choices

You may update your account information, unsubscribe from emails, or opt out of text alerts at any time by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your personal information, contact us at the email below.
